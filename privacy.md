# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 20, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Relay ("the app," "we") handles your information. Relay is an iOS application for tracking sleep sessions during the newborn period.

The short version: **Relay collects no personal data. None.**

The rest of this document explains that in the specific detail Apple's App Store guidelines and applicable privacy regulations require.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**We collect no personal information.** Relay does not ask for, transmit, or store any of the following:

- Name, email, phone number, or any account identifier
- Location data (GPS, IP-based, or otherwise)
- Contacts, calendar, photos, or any other system data
- Health or fitness data (Relay does not connect to HealthKit)
- Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or analytics identifiers
- Crash logs or telemetry of any kind
- Usage data, behavioral data, or interaction events

Relay has no account system. There is nothing to sign in to, nothing to register for, and no user profile to create.

## 2. Information Stored On Your Device

Relay stores the following information **locally on your device** using Apple's SwiftData framework:

- Sleep session records (start time, end time, which parent, and an optional note you may type)
- The display names you enter for each parent in the app's settings
- App preferences (e.g., which widget configuration you've chosen)

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to enable iCloud backup (see Section 3).

## 3. iCloud Backup

If — and only if — you have iCloud backup enabled in your iPhone's Settings (Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud), Apple will back up the data stored on your device, including Relay's local database, to your personal iCloud account.

This backup is controlled by **Apple**, governed by Apple's privacy policies, and accessible only to you through your iCloud account. The developer of Relay has no access to your iCloud backups and cannot read, modify, or retrieve them.

You can disable iCloud backup for Relay at any time in iOS Settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Relay uses **no third-party services**. Specifically:

- No analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Segment, etc.)
- No advertising SDKs and no advertising of any kind
- No crash-reporting services
- No remote logging services
- No social media SDKs

The app does not contact any server controlled by the developer or any third party.

## 5. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not use any information. The data you enter into the app is used only to display the app's screens to you on your own device.

## 6. How We Share Information

We do not share any information, because we do not collect any. The developer has no access to anything you enter into Relay.

## 7. Data Retention

Information you enter in Relay is retained on your device until you delete it (either by deleting individual sessions in the app, by tapping "Wipe all data" in the app's debug settings if available, or by uninstalling the app).

If iCloud backup is enabled, retention of the backup is governed by Apple's iCloud retention policies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Relay does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The app is designed for adult caregivers (parents) of newborns.

Relay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 9. Security

Information stored on your device is protected by the standard iOS data-protection mechanisms. The app does not transmit data to any server, so there is no transmission-security concern. iCloud backups (if enabled) are encrypted by Apple.

## 10. Your Rights

Because Relay does not collect or store any information off your device, there is no developer-held data for you to request, correct, delete, or port. All data you enter is in your control, on your device.

To delete all data Relay has stored on your device, either:

1. Delete individual sessions in the app's "Edit" tab, OR
2. Uninstall the app — iOS will remove the app's local data when you do.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in any material way, the change will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top, and a notice will appear in the next version of the app's release notes.
